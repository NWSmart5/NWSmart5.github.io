Why Safe AI Matters

AI can transform manufacturing, but it also introduces new risks. Following official NCSC guidance helps you adopt AI securely and responsibly.

The 4 Key Stages of Secure AI

Adapted for manufacturing SMEs from the official NCSC Guidelines.

Understand threats early. Perform risk assessments and design with security and human oversight in mind.

SME Tip: Demand threat models from vendors and maintain human control on the factory floor.

Protect your data and models. Require proper documentation (Model Cards, SBOMs) from suppliers.

Use strong access controls, testing, and incident response plans before going live in production.

Monitor performance, detect anomalies, and plan secure updates throughout the AI system's life.

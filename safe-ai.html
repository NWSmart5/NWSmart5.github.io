The 4 Key Stages of Secure AI (Adapted for Manufacturing SMEs)

Focus: Understand risks early and make informed design choices before adopting or buying AI.

  • Raise awareness of AI-specific threats (prompt injection, data poisoning, adversarial attacks)
  • Conduct holistic threat modelling — consider impact on production, safety, and proprietary data
  • Choose AI only when appropriate and apply least privilege, guardrails, and human oversight
  • Prioritise explainable models for quality inspection and predictive maintenance

SME Takeaway: Always ask vendors for threat models and evidence of secure design. Plan human oversight for factory-floor AI.

Focus: Secure your supply chain and protect valuable manufacturing data.

  • Vet suppliers of models, data, and APIs
  • Protect proprietary factory data when fine-tuning models
  • Demand documentation (Model Cards, SBOMs)
  • Manage technical debt and have decommissioning plans

SME Takeaway: Treat your manufacturing data as highly sensitive. Require full documentation before integration.

Focus: Launch AI safely into your production environment.

  • Apply strong access controls and environment segregation
  • Protect models from tampering or theft
  • Prepare AI-specific incident response plans
  • Test thoroughly in conditions that match your factory

SME Takeaway: Always have manual overrides ready for any AI controlling physical equipment.

Focus: Keep the AI secure throughout its lifetime.

  • Monitor inputs, outputs, and performance for drift or attacks
  • Implement secure update processes
  • Log and learn from incidents
  • Share anonymised lessons with industry

SME Takeaway: Treat major model updates with the same caution as installing new machinery.

Cybersecurity for Manufacturing SMEs

Secure operations. Resilient systems. Peace of mind.

Why Cybersecurity Matters for North West Manufacturers

Manufacturing is a high-value target for cyber threats. As you adopt IoT, AI, automation, and connected systems, strong cybersecurity is essential for protecting production, data, people, and reputation.

  • Cyber attacks on manufacturing have risen sharply (ransomware, supply chain attacks, OT/ICS breaches).
  • Attacks can stop production lines, leak intellectual property, or cause physical damage.
  • UK manufacturing faces skills shortages and longer recruitment times for cyber roles.
  • Industry 5.0 increases connectivity — bringing both opportunity and risk.

Good cybersecurity protects your competitiveness, not just your systems.

Cyber Governance for Boards

Source: NCSC Cyber Governance for Boards guidance (ncsc.gov.uk).

Cyber risk is a principal business risk. Boards and directors do not need to be technical experts, but they must understand cyber governance principles so they can ask the right questions, evaluate preparedness, and ensure cyber security measures align with business goals.

Key Principles of Cyber Governance (NCSC Code of Practice)

Identify and understand cyber risks to your organisation, including OT/ICS systems and supply chain risks.

Integrate cyber security into your overall business strategy and ensure board-level ownership.

Build a security-aware culture and ensure staff at all levels understand their responsibilities.

Regularly review cyber security performance and hold management accountable.

Ensure robust plans are in place to respond to and recover from cyber incidents.

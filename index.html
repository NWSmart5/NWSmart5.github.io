What is NWSmart5.0?

NWSmart5.0 is a regional initiative supporting manufacturing Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) across Greater Manchester and the North West to navigate the transition towards Industry 5.0.

Whilst Industry 4.0 introduced digital technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and automation, Industry 5.0 emphasises more than just technology. It focuses on human-centric innovation, sustainability, and resilience — all critical for future competitiveness.

The project addresses key barriers faced by manufacturing SMEs, including technological complexity, limited accessible knowledge, and uncertainty around secure and sustainable implementation.

Project Vision

The vision of NWSmart5.0 is to empower manufacturing Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) in the North West to lead the next era of industrial transformation — one that is secure, smart, sustainable, and human-centric.

NWSmart5.0 seeks to bridge knowledge gaps, strengthen cyber resilience, and support the responsible adoption of AI and digital technologies in alignment with Industry 5.0 principles.

What is Industry 5.0?

Industry 5.0 is the natural evolution of Industry 4.0. While Industry 4.0 brought digitalisation, automation, IoT, and AI for greater efficiency, Industry 5.0 goes further by placing people, sustainability, and resilience at the core of industrial transformation.

“Industry 5.0 is a sustainable, human-centric and resilient industry… It moves the focus from shareholder to stakeholder value… placing the wellbeing of the industry worker at the centre of the production process.”

The Three Core Pillars:

Technology serves people. Workers move from repetitive tasks to more creative, supervisory, and fulfilling roles. Emphasis on wellbeing, inclusion, diversity, and lifelong learning.

Production respects planetary boundaries — supporting the circular economy, resource efficiency, renewable energy, and the European Green Deal.

Building robust systems and supply chains that can withstand shocks such as cyber-attacks, pandemics, geopolitical issues, and climate events.

Why This Matters for North West SMEs

Manufacturing SMEs face real barriers — technological complexity, skills gaps, and uncertainty around secure implementation.

This Knowledge Hub bridges those gaps with practical, actionable resources so you can adopt smart technologies securely, responsibly, and sustainably.

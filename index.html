What is Industry 5.0?

Industry 5.0 is the natural evolution of Industry 4.0. While Industry 4.0 brought digitalisation, automation, IoT, and AI for greater efficiency, Industry 5.0 goes further by placing people, sustainability, and resilience at the core of industrial transformation.

“Industry 5.0 is a sustainable, human-centric and resilient European industry… It moves the focus from shareholder to stakeholder value… placing the wellbeing of the industry worker at the centre of the production process.”

Source: Breque, M., De Nul, L., & Petridis, A. (2021). Industry 5.0: Towards a sustainable, human-centric and resilient European industry. European Commission.

The Three Core Pillars:

Technology serves people. Workers move from repetitive tasks to more creative, supervisory, and fulfilling roles. Emphasis on wellbeing, inclusion, diversity, and lifelong learning.

Production respects planetary boundaries — supporting the circular economy, resource efficiency, renewable energy, and the European Green Deal.

Building robust systems and supply chains that can withstand shocks such as cyber-attacks, pandemics, geopolitical issues, and climate events.

Why This Matters for North West SMEs

Manufacturing SMEs face real barriers — technological complexity, skills gaps, and uncertainty around secure implementation.

This Knowledge Hub bridges those gaps with practical, actionable resources so you can adopt smart technologies securely, responsibly, and sustainably.

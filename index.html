Welcome to the NWSmart5.0 Knowledge Hub

Your free, open-access resource centre dedicated to helping manufacturing SMEs in Greater Manchester and the North West thrive in Industry 5.0.

We combine practical guidance on safe AI, cybersecurity, and digital transformation with a strong focus on human-centric, sustainable, and resilient practices.

What is Industry 5.0?

Industry 5.0 goes beyond automation by placing people, sustainability, and resilience at the centre of industrial transformation.

Technology serves people. Workers move from repetitive tasks to more creative, supervisory, and fulfilling roles. Emphasis on wellbeing, inclusion, diversity, and lifelong learning.

Production respects planetary boundaries and supports the circular economy, resource efficiency, renewable energy, and the European Green Deal.

Building robust systems and supply chains that can withstand shocks such as cyber-attacks, pandemics, geopolitical issues, and climate events.
